Privacy Policy

App: From Payday to Payday
Last updated: October 6, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information is processed in the From Payday to Payday app and how the app uses advertising services.

1. Publisher

The app is published by Pawel. For privacy-related questions, you can use the repository page: GitHub.

2. Data entered in the app

The app lets users enter budget data such as payday amount, payday day, expenses, categories, notes, and payday additions. This data is stored locally on the user's device and is used to display the budget, calendar, and forecast.

The app does not require a user account and does not send user-entered budget data to the app publisher's server.

3. Ads and Google AdMob

The app uses Google Mobile Ads SDK / Google AdMob to display banner ads and interstitial ads. As a result, Google may automatically process data needed for ad delivery, fraud prevention, ad analytics, and diagnostics.

Data processed by Google may include, among other things: IP address, device advertising ID, device information, ad interactions, and diagnostic information. For details, see Google's documentation: Google Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure and Google Privacy Policy.

4. App permissions

The app may use internet access and network state access to load ads. The app may also use the advertising ID in accordance with Google Play policies.

5. Data sharing

The app publisher does not sell user-entered budget data. Advertising-related data may be processed by Google as the advertising service provider according to Google's policies.

6. Security and storage

Budget data entered by the user is stored locally on the device. Uninstalling the app may delete locally stored app data.

7. Children

The app is not specifically directed to children. If the app is published for selected age groups on Google Play, audience and ad settings should comply with Google Play and AdMob policies.

8. Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as the app evolves or as legal requirements and Google Play policies change. The current version will be available at this address.
